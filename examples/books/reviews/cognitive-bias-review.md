# Cognitive Biases & Heuristics Synthesis

## Overview
This synthesis examines the behavioral economic models formulated by [daniel-kahneman](../authors/daniel-kahneman.md) in [kahneman-fast-slow](../books/kahneman-fast-slow.md).

## Key Takeaways
1. **Anchoring Effect**: Initial values disproportionately skew subsequent estimates.
2. **Loss Aversion**: Losses hurt roughly twice as intensely as gains feel rewarding.
